# Mezzanail Nail Studio Official — Velvet Precision

## Brand direction
Mezzanail Nail Studio is a modern luxury nail studio in Malaysia. The official website uses editorial clarity, architectural spacing and quiet confidence. It should feel personal, precise and trustworthy—never cute, crowded or dependent on a third-party platform identity.

## Visual system
- Black `#050505`: authority, editorial sections and footer
- White `#FFFFFF`: primary canvas and clarity
- Soft grey `#F4F4F2`: section contrast
- Champagne gold `#C8A96B`: appointment CTAs and small brand accents only
- Manrope: all display, interface and multilingual text
- 16–20px radii, fine borders, soft shadows and generous whitespace

## Interaction system
- Mobile first; fixed bottom navigation for Home, Services, Promotions and Booking
- Every major page exposes a direct appointment CTA in one or two steps
- 220–600ms deliberate transitions, subtle scroll reveal and 4–6px card lift
- All motion respects reduced-motion preferences; no looping decoration

## Content and link rules
- All booking CTAs read from `lib/site.ts` and never display the raw URL or provider name
- App promotion shows only Mezzanail Nail Studio branding and official download buttons
- Maps, WhatsApp, review and social destinations are centralised in `lib/site.ts`
- Google Reviews supports a configured embed URL with a lightweight review-card fallback

## Avoid
- Pink salon clichés, hearts, bows, cartoon motifs or candy pastels
- Excessive metallic gradients, ornamental frames, noisy VIP badges or fake scarcity
- Third-party booking branding, exposed integration URLs or scattered hard-coded external links
