# Data retention and privacy request runbook

This internal runbook supports the public Privacy Policy and Job Applicant
Privacy Notice. It is an operating checklist, not public-facing legal copy.

## Monthly retention review

Complete the review on the first business day of each month. Record only the
review date, reviewer, system, date range removed and aggregate record count.
Do not copy customer or applicant data into the deletion log.

| Record category | Standard maximum period | Required action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| General enquiries and service correspondence | 12 months after last interaction | Delete or anonymise messages that are no longer needed |
| Booking, payment and transaction records | 7 years where required | Restrict access; delete non-statutory attachments and duplicates earlier |
| Membership profile and service history | Active membership plus 24 months | Close or anonymise inactive profiles; keep statutory transaction records separately |
| Promotion entries and winner administration | 12 months after prize fulfilment | Delete entry exports, verification copies and contact lists |
| Unsuccessful job applications | 12 months from submission | Delete the recruitment email, PDF attachment and corresponding database row |
| Failed or abandoned job submissions | 30 days after the last retry need ends | Delete the database row and any provider failure copy |
| Security and rate-limiting records | 90 days | Delete unless an active abuse or legal investigation requires a documented hold |
| Google Analytics user and event data | Up to 14 months | Keep the GA4 property retention setting at no more than 14 months |

When a legal, tax, fraud, dispute or safety hold applies, document the category,
reason, approving person and next review date. Remove the hold as soon as the
reason ends.

## Privacy requests

1. Accept written requests delivered through the studio WhatsApp or postal
   address and save the request date.
2. Ask for only proportionate verification. Do not request an identity document
   unless the record cannot otherwise be located safely.
3. Search the systems relevant to the request: studio messages, Tunai booking or
   membership records, promotion records, recruitment inbox, job application
   database and analytics records where identifiable.
4. Apply access, correction, consent withdrawal, marketing opt-out or deletion
   as requested, subject to documented legal exceptions.
5. Respond within the period required by applicable Malaysian law and retain a
   minimal audit of the request outcome.

For job applicants, use the application reference whenever available. Deleting
only the database row is incomplete: the recruitment email and PDF attachment
must also be removed from the controlled inbox and its Trash or retention
system.

## Analytics controls

- Google Analytics must not load before the browser records a `granted` choice.
- Advertising signals and advertising personalisation must remain disabled.
- The public Cookies & Analytics page must continue to provide a working
  settings button.
- Recheck the public notice whenever the measurement ID, provider, cookie
  duration, retention setting or collected event fields change.
